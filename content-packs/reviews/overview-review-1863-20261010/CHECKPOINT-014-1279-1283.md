## 1279. I am not quite convinced

- meaning_type: Conversational expression of doubt: politely says that the argument has not fully persuaded you.
- connotation: Softens disagreement; 'not quite' leaves room for further evidence or explanation.
- tone: Polite and cautious here: the speaker doubts whether a shorter trial will supply enough evidence.
- register: Neutral to professional; also usable in ordinary planning and discussion.
- Tamil: எனக்கு இன்னும் முழுமையாக நம்பிக்கை ஏற்படவில்லை என்று மரியாதையாகக் கூறுதல்
- Pronunciation: /aɪ əm nɒt kwaɪt kənˈvɪnst/

## 1280. somewhat

- meaning_type: Abstract degree modifier: partly or to a limited degree, here describing the neighbour's irritability.
- connotation: Neutral by itself; softens the negative description 'crotchety'.
- tone: Lightly teasing here: the narrator tempers the description of the neighbour while calling him colourful.
- register: Neutral to slightly formal; 'a bit' is more conversational.
- Tamil: ஓரளவு; மிக அதிகமாக அல்ல
- Pronunciation: Approximate pronunciation: SUM-wut. Capitals show stress; this is text guidance, not audio.

## 1281. union

- meaning_type: Literal organization: a group of workers that represents their shared rights and interests.
- connotation: Neutral as an organizational term; the disappointing contract does not make union itself a negative label.
- tone: Matter-of-fact but critical of the outcome here: 'worse' contrasts the settlement with the original demands.
- register: Neutral; common in workplace, labour and news discussion.
- Tamil: தொழிலாளர்களின் உரிமைகளையும் நலன்களையும் பாதுகாக்கும் தொழிற்சங்கம்
- Pronunciation: Approximate pronunciation: YOON-yuhn. Capitals show stress; this is text guidance, not audio.

## 1282. with immediate effect

- meaning_type: Time expression: a rule or decision starts applying now.
- connotation: Neutral; specifies the start time without judging the change.
- tone: Formal, decisive notice.
- register: Formal; common in official announcements and policy notices.
- Tamil: உடனடியாக அமலுக்கு வரும் வகையில்
- Pronunciation: /wɪð ɪˌmiːdiət ɪˈfekt/

## 1283. trade-off

- meaning_type: Abstract choice: getting one benefit requires giving up another.
- connotation: Usually neutral about competing benefits; 'simplistic' criticizes the particular trade-offs described here.
- tone: Critical here: the writer says these choices limit creative thinking and justify unhelpful behaviour.
- register: Neutral; common in planning, business and informed everyday discussion.
- Tamil: ஒரு நன்மையைப் பெறுவதற்காக மற்றொரு நன்மையை விட்டுக்கொடுக்கும் சமரசம்
- Pronunciation: Approximate pronunciation: TRAYD-awf. Capitals show stress; this is text guidance, not audio.
