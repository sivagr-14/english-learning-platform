## 1307. with that caveat

- meaning_type: Logical qualification: asks the reader to keep the stated limitation in mind when judging the result.
- connotation: Neutral and careful; limits confidence without rejecting the results.
- tone: Cautiously positive here: the small sample is acknowledged before the results are called promising.
- register: Formal; natural in reports and careful discussion. 'With that limitation in mind' is plainer.
- Tamil: அந்த வரம்பை மனதில் கொண்டு
- Pronunciation: /wɪð ðæt ˈkæv.i.æt/
