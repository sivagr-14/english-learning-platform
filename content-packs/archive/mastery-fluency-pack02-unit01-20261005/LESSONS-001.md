## pack02-0001 · actionable

/ˈæk.ʃən.ə.bəl/ · adjective

**Tamil:** செயல்படுத்தக்கூடிய

### Overview

**Meaning Usage Profile**

**Meaning Type:** Contextual meaning: specific enough to be acted on usefully

**Connotation:** Neutral, practical. Useful information may help understanding; actionable information suggests what to do next.

**Tone:** Neutral, practical.

**Register:** Usable in clear general or professional communication. In workplace discussion, actionable describes practical usability; it does not promise that a recommendation is correct.

### Meaning In Context

**Source Sentence:** Please turn the survey findings into actionable recommendations.

**Contextual Meaning:** specific enough to be acted on usefully

**Simple Explanation:** Specific enough to be acted on usefully. Picture feedback with a clear next step attached.

### Usage Guide

**When To Use**

- Turn a vague complaint about a meeting into one actionable recommendation.
- A natural model: The coach gave me actionable feedback on my opening paragraph.

**When Not To Use**

- Useful information may help understanding; actionable information suggests what to do next.
- In workplace discussion, actionable describes practical usability; it does not promise that a recommendation is correct.

### Patterns Collocations

**Main Pattern:** actionable: actionable + feedback/recommendations/insights

**Common Collocations**

- actionable feedback
- actionable recommendations

### Natural Examples

**Examples**

**Scenario:** Please turn the survey findings into actionable recommendations.

**Another Setting:** The coach gave me actionable feedback on my opening paragraph.

**Mini Conversation:** A: The coach gave me actionable feedback on my opening paragraph.
B: What did she suggest changing?

**Important Difference:** Useful information may help understanding; actionable information suggests what to do next.
