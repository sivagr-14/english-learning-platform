# Mastery Skills — pack02 progress

Target: 400 new entries. Generated, structurally validated and delivered in this commit: 100. Remaining: 300. This manifest freezes only the first 100; it does not claim the other 300 have been selected or assessed. Local sync is deferred.

The 1,363-entry private account baseline and all 400 pilot01 candidate senses were considered for duplicates. The private account export is not included. Same spelling with a genuinely distinct meaning remains eligible in future units, but a pronoun variant of an existing expression is not treated as a new sense.

Scope of this unit: workplace authority and decisions, clear official messages, everyday choices, emotional nuance and learning. No technical glossary padding. All 100 lessons include eight simplified-v2 sections, two examples, a matched dialogue, Tamil gloss, UK IPA, patterns, collocations, a correction, a contrast, nuance, a memory cue and retrieval/recognition/production practice.

Suggested practice: read the dialogue; hide the entry and recall the expression from its meaning; say your own response; revisit after roughly 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days, shortening the interval after a difficult recall. This is a suggested study routine, not a claim that app scheduling was changed.

Next 300 remain work to do. Planned coverage: everyday requests and service interactions; workplace collaboration and diplomatic responses; precise explanation and storytelling. These are coverage intentions, not frozen candidate lists.
