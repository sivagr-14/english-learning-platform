# Author quality review

100 new lessons reviewed; this is an author review, not an independent linguistic certification. Production schema validation is recorded separately in VALIDATION.json.

- Meaning and scenario match the frozen candidate exactly; scenarios are authored.
- Two distinct example situations appear in every entry. Each dialogue's opening is the second example, with a context-specific reply reviewed for speaker coherence.
- All eight sections are present. Every entry has UK IPA, a Tamil gloss, a reusable pattern, two collocations or phrase frames, an error/correction, a specific contrast and usage nuance.
- Retrieval prompts begin from the meaning; production prompts require a new response. Recognition answers are balanced: 50 A and 50 B. These are static lesson materials, not a claim of new interactive app behaviour.
- The spelling/pronoun variant meet someone halfway was rejected because meet halfway was already covered. Similar communicative functions are not automatically duplicates: e.g. learn the ropes and show someone the ropes teach different participant roles and grammar, with an explicit contrast.
- Do not present CEFR or frequency as corpus-certified ratings. The selection favours practical contemporary B1–C1 communication; uncommon jargon, obsolete vocabulary and fabricated expressions are not used to fill a quota.
- Local import remains deferred. The planned visible pack is still 100/400 generated; the next 300 have no frozen membership in this unit manifest.
